Background music
================
The 3 tracks here (soft-glow, lofi-blush, morning-light) were generated from scratch by
tools/make_music.py - they are copyright-free, use them anywhere.

Want different music? Drop .mp3 files in this folder; one is picked at random each day.
Only use music you are allowed to use, for example:
  * YouTube Audio Library (studio.youtube.com > Audio library) - tracks marked "no attribution required"
  * Pixabay Music (pixabay.com/music) - free for commercial use
Do NOT use Bollywood / trending songs - Instagram and YouTube will mute or block the reel.
Tip: trending Instagram audio can only be added inside the Instagram app, not by any software.
