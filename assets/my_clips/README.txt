Your own clips (optional)
=========================
Put your own vertical videos or photos here (.mp4, .mov, .jpg, .png).
Then in config.yaml set:   background_source: "my_clips"   (only yours)
                       or  background_source: "mixed"      (yours first, then Pexels)
Faceless clips work best: hands, products, mirror shots, flat-lays.
Note: GitHub allows files up to 100 MB each - keep clips short (5-15 s).
